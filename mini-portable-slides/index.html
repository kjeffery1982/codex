Prototype name: Comet Cove Dream Slide

What it is

A portable slide imagined by a kid who wanted every trip down to feel like blasting off.

It is not just a slide. It is a launch deck, a clubhouse, a hiding spot, a glowing mission tunnel, and a place to invent rules only kids understand. The whole idea is that the product feels magical before anyone even climbs up.

Core promise

Every part should make a kid say, “Wait, it can do that too?”

What makes it amazing

The dream features a fifth grader would absolutely insist on keeping.

Glow-Rail Comet Lights

The rails shine in little star dots at night so the slide feels like a spaceship runway instead of plain plastic.

Secret Cove Underneath

The underside turns into a hidden mini fort with a round hatch door for maps, snacks, and whispered plans.

Cloud-Step Climb

The ladder steps feel bouncy and soft-looking, like climbing tiny cartoon clouds on the way to the launch deck.

Mission Buttons

Press one for countdown mode, one for treasure mode, and one for “super sneaky” mode with quieter lights.

Rocket-Fin Sides

The side wings make the whole shape feel fast even when it is standing still in the room waiting for the next turn.

Adventure Fold

When playtime is over, it folds into a neat glowing shell, like a spaceship parking itself until tomorrow.

Dream blueprint

Three big ideas built into one signature slide.

Launch Deck A high little platform with a countdown arch and a bright “go” light.
Speed Run A smooth wide lane meant to feel fast, safe, and extra exciting at the bottom.
Hideout Base A mini cove underneath for quiet plotting, toy storage, and fort-building energy.
