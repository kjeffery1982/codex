Why this category is broken

Parents currently choose between movement for their kids and visual sanity for their homes.

The current market splits into two bad options: cheap bright plastic that dominates the room, or bulky play structures that never disappear. OrbitSlide One is built around a more valuable idea: a slide system that earns the right to stay in a beautiful home.

Commodity toys feel disposable

Most indoor slides look temporary, wobble under use, and tell parents to compromise on design.

Large play gear never really stores

“Foldable” products still eat floor space, look chaotic, and become permanent room roommates.

Kids outgrow the magic fast

Single-function slides are exciting once, then become furniture-shaped background noise.

Product thesis

The first indoor slide system designed with the logic of modular furniture, not outdoor playground leftovers.

That means fewer parts, calmer materials, and a silhouette that feels intentional from every angle. The kid payoff is still immediate: climb, perch, hide, slide, repeat. The parent payoff is equally strong: it stores quickly and does not make the whole room look surrendered.
