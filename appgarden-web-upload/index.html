How It's Made

Briar and her group of creative entrepreneurial friends make every piece together.

Briar, B-R-I-A-R, sketches the first ideas, then her friends fold, weave, sort colors, and test each design until it feels special enough for the shop table. They treat every item like a tiny handmade gift.
